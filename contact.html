About StudentHub

StudentHub is a centralized web portal built for a single campus community — students, faculty and administrators — to manage academic activity from one place instead of scattered notices, spreadsheets and group chats.

Why this project exists

Most colleges rely on a mix of printed notices, WhatsApp groups and email to share information that changes every week. StudentHub replaces that with a single portal where every role sees exactly what's relevant to them.

Who uses StudentHub

Students
View notices, timetables and study material; submit assignments; check attendance and results.
Faculty
Upload study material and assignments, post notices, and manage attendance for their subjects.
Administrators
Manage user accounts and approve notices and events across the portal.
Guests
Browse public pages such as About, Events and Contact without signing in.

About this build

This site is being developed incrementally as part of a Web Development Framework course, starting with semantic, accessible HTML5 page structures and growing to include styling, interactivity and backend features in later stages.
