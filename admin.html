Frequently asked questions

Who can create a StudentHub account?
Any current student or faculty member with a valid institute email address can register. Faculty accounts are verified by an administrator before activation.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
Use the "Forgot your password?" link on the login page to reset it using your registered email address.
How often is attendance updated?
Faculty mark attendance after each class, and it usually reflects on your dashboard within the same day.
Can I submit assignments after the deadline?
Late submissions depend on your instructor's policy. Contact your faculty member directly if you need an extension.
Who do I contact for technical problems?
Use the Contact page or email support@studenthub.edu.
